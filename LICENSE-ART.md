## What this covers ("the Joyrings Assets")

- **Ring artwork**: every image and video of a ring model, including
  `apps/web/public/rings/**`, `.artwork/**`, the files pinned to IPFS and
  listed in `artwork.json`, and any future animations.
- **The ring designs themselves**, including the toy-3D rings drawn by
  `apps/web/src/lib/ring-art.ts` and rasterized by
  `scripts/render-ring-art.mjs`. The code is MIT; the pictures it draws, used
  as Joyrings artwork, are covered here.
- **The brand**: the name "Joyrings", the ring logo, the "Certificate of Joy"
  design, the share-card designs, and the visual identity (the toy-3D style
  together with the candy-rose palette) as used for Joyrings.
- **Marketing materials**: pitch decks, demo videos and social cards,
  wherever they are published.
